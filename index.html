Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed insights into our driver architecture, wireless protocols, and global listening trials.

Standard dynamic headphones utilize a voice coil attached to a conical dome, which causes cone flex and modal distortion at high sound pressure levels. Aurora Signal’s Planar-Flux™ diaphragm is a sub-micron beryllium membrane with a micro-etched conductive trace driven uniformly across the entire surface by symmetric neodymium magnets. This eliminates surface distortion, delivering transient speeds of under 0.02ms.
Unlike consumer Bluetooth codecs (AAC, LDAC, aptX) that compress audio bandwidth to 990kbps, AuroraLink™ employs dedicated 2.4GHz ultra-wideband RF transmission at 8.4 Mbps with adaptive channel-hopping. This allows raw, uncompressed 192kHz/32-bit linear PCM audio to be streamed with under 0.8ms latency—faster than the human auditory cortex can register.
